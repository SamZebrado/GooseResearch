Give yourself a moment
after learning.

You don’t have to start the next thing right away. Breathe naturally and notice how you feel and what’s around you. Acknowledge your effort and the help you received. Then leave a quick note: what did you learn, and how long did you spend?

This is a public template, not a private journal. Every sample comes from a fictional alien research project and contains no real people, dates, or journal entries. The demo shows a way to keep notes; it does not promise better health or learning outcomes.

01 · Pause, then make a note

Breathe naturally. Notice your shoulders, your feet, and what’s around you. You might thank yourself for finishing a small step or thank someone who helped. It’s fine to skip this.

Don’t enter private notes in this public demo. Entries live only in memory. The page neither reads existing browser data nor uploads anything. Reloading or closing it clears your entries.

02 · Notice your effort

Only the study time you enter counts, not breaks. You can log several sessions on the same topic. Entry numbers are not timestamps.

The two built-in fictional examples cannot be undone. Try a sample to see how it works.

03 · Pomodoro-style notes: log time and work separately

Pick a small goal and a focus interval that suits you. When you stop, note the actual minutes and what you did, then take a break. If there’s more to do, mark it “to be continued.” There’s no need to push through just to fill the time.

Fictional example: spend 20 minutes on a diagram of alien plant sounds, pause and breathe naturally, then write “I can tell two signals apart. I’ll look at the third next time.” Use the note for reflection and thanks, and the timer for the interval. Don’t count the same session twice.

04 · Jot down a quick note

Use fictional content only. Write **bold** text, use Cmd / Ctrl + B, or save with Cmd / Ctrl + Enter. Enter starts a new line; confirming text in an input method does not submit. HTML and links are displayed as plain text. Type 【 or place the cursor inside 【】 to pick a tag from this session.

After closing a tag, you can insert a duration template with the cursor ready for the number. “【Tag】Spent 25 minutes” counts whether or not it is bold. Only the first explicit duration counts; don’t log it again in the form above. Notes without a duration are welcome. The note uses the Shanghai time when you first start typing; clearing it resets that time. Backdated entries also show when you began the note. Submission time is shown separately. These timestamps are only for this browser demo.

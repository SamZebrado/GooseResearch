让 AI 按你指定的格式导出文档

批注审完后，可以请 AI 把当前版本整理成你要的回复信或文档。这是一份操作指南；静态演示不会调用 AI、生成文件或上传材料。

  1. 给模板和定稿范围。在你有权限使用的私有环境中，提供指定的空白模板或格式说明，以及选定的当前稿件。说明导出范围、版本和目标格式，例如 DOCX。不要把整个讨论记录当正文。
  2. 交代哪些要保留。先套标题、编号和段落样式，再保留原有的图、表、图注和编号。未解决的占位符原样保留；只有明确要求保留为批注的决策，才在目标格式支持时写成原生批注。旁边的讨论气泡不进正文，也不复制为批注。
  3. 先验稿，再分享。让 AI 另存一份私有草稿，打开核对章节、图表、图注、占位符和批注锚点。若格式或工具不支持原生批注，让 AI 在交付说明另列缺项，不要擅自改成正文、上传或发邮件。

完全虚构的示例：《月球温室折光记录》回复信使用“意见 → 回复 → 修改位置”。[待核：示意图编号] 留在原处，“保留图注原文”是明确指定的批注，旁边的讨论不导出。

可直接复制的提示词

用我给的模板，把我选定的当前版本导出成指定格式的私有草稿。按模板组织标题、编号和段落，保留图、表、图注及其编号；未解决的标记一字不改。仅把我明确指定的决策写成原生批注（工具支持时），不要带上讨论气泡。不支持的项目另列，不写进正文。请检查导出文件后再交给我，不覆盖原件，不上传、不分享、不发邮件。

Ask AI to export a document in your own format

Once you’ve reviewed the feedback, have AI format the current version as a response letter or document. This is a how-to guide; the static demo does not call AI, generate files, or upload material.

  1. Provide the template and scope. In an authorized private workspace, supply the blank template or formatting instructions and the selected current text. Specify the sections, version, and output format, such as DOCX. Do not treat the full discussion as manuscript text.
  2. Say what must survive. Apply the template’s headings, numbering, and paragraph styles. Preserve figures, tables, captions, and their numbering. Keep unresolved markers exactly as written. Include only decisions explicitly marked for export as native document comments where supported. Leave discussion bubbles out of both the text and the comments.
  3. Check the draft first. Save a separate private draft. Open it to check sections, figures, tables, captions, markers, and comment anchors. List any unsupported items in the handoff, not in the document body. Do not upload it or email it automatically.

Fully fictional example: For Refraction Notes from a Lunar Greenhouse, use “Comment → Response → Location of change.” Keep [CHECK: diagram number] as written. Add “Keep the original caption” only as an explicitly requested comment, and omit the surrounding discussion.

Copyable prompt

Use the supplied template to export my selected current version as a separate private draft in the specified format. Match the template’s headings, numbering, and paragraph styles. Preserve figures, tables, captions, and numbering. Keep unresolved markers exactly as written. Add only decisions I explicitly listed as native comments if supported. Exclude discussion bubbles.
